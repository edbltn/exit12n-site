Exit 12N: what the map says so far

Reading the map. Fetching the live data.

1The line

 

The exit is drawn straight, with descriptive location names along it; the rise, the crest and the descent. Places along it are given as stations, the way a road is measured: 0+463 is 463 meters from where the exit leaves the parkway. A band's thickness represents the park's width, from the exit across to the park's far edge, so at every point the bands above the line and the bands below it add up to the same full width. A bubble is a proposed thing along the exit, stacked away from the line to avoid overlap; proposals with the same name standing within 40 meters of each other are one bubble with their count beside it (different names never merge), and its hover names them and adds their votes: its colour is its type's colour on the map, its area is its footprint at the bands' scale, and it is more opaque the more votes for it it has, net of those against. Only paths are bands: each proposed path's ground is taken out of the land cover it is drawn on, and only the part inside the park contributes. A proposal to remove something that is there is neither: the ground it covers turns to grass below the line, unless a shown path takes it, and a hover on that grass names the removal. Hover or tap anything to see what it is.
