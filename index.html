Exit 12N

The off-ramp from the Henry Hudson Parkway at the foot of Riverside Park has been closed to cars since July 2025. Neighbours have been walking and biking into the park through it ever since. What should it become?

Open the map and vote

 

What this is

Exit 12N is a map you can use on a phone or a laptop. It shows the ramp and the streets and the park around it in 3D, as they are today. You can place something on it, such as a garden, a bench, a ball court or a bike lane, or draw a path, and you can add your vote to what others have drawn. A drawing with many votes behind it is easy to see, and so is one that only a few people want.

The map does not decide anything. It shows what people would like to see, so that the people who do decide, and everyone else, can look at it together.

How it came about

People near the ramp had been organizing for it before we arrived. Neighbours keep a website about it and a petition is open, and the community board's parks committee heard the neighbours' plan in February 2025. Not everyone wants the same thing: some neighbours would like to see the ramp reopened to cars, others would like it to stay closed to cars and open to people on foot and on bikes. The community board's parks committee has said it will not take a position until it has heard both sides.

In October 2026 we, Brook Constantz and Eric Bolton of Spherical Harmonics, wrote to the neighbours, the community board and others who were already working on it, and offered a way for more people to take part: a shared map anyone could draw on. On November 4 we held an online workshop on it. On Sunday, November 22, neighbours and street advocacy groups held a public walk from 116th Street and Riverside Drive to the ramp, with thirty-one people registered. Afterwards we held a workshop to work on the map together.

We plan to bring what the map shows to Community Board 9 in December.

What the map says so far

The results page reads the live votes and shows them along the length of the ramp, one kind of neighbour at a time: someone who walks, someone who climbs, someone who gardens, someone who plays ball. It updates as people vote.

Add your voice

Open the map, find a drawing and press the plus or the minus. You do not need an account. If you want to draw something of your own, use the add button on the map and choose what to put down. You can take a vote back later.
