The question

The off-ramp from the Henry Hudson Parkway at the foot of Riverside Park has been closed to cars since July 2025. Neighbours have been walking and biking into the park through it ever since. What should it become?

Contents
§1

The ramp Where things stand

People near the ramp had been organizing for it before we arrived. Neighbours keep a website about it and a petition is open, and the community board's parks committee heard the neighbours' plan in February 2025.

Not everyone wants the same thing: some neighbours would like to see the ramp reopened to cars, others would like it to stay closed to cars and open to people on foot and on bikes. The community board's parks committee has said it will not take a position until it has heard both sides.

§2

The map Draw a proposal, vote on others

Exit 12N is a map you can use on a phone or a laptop. It shows the ramp and the streets and the park around it in 3D, as they are today. You can place something on it, such as a garden, a bench, a ball court or a bike lane, or draw a path, and you can add your vote to what others have drawn. A drawing with many votes behind it is easy to see, and so is one that only a few people want.

The map does not decide anything. It shows what people would like to see, so that the people who do decide, and everyone else, can look at it together.

To add your voice, open the map, find a drawing and press the plus or the minus. You do not need an account. If you want to draw something of your own, use the add button on the map and choose what to put down. You can take a vote back later.

§4

Representatives Who decides, and how to write to them

The map shows what neighbours would like. The people who decide about the ramp are elected officials and the city's transportation department, and they hear from you directly too. Write whatever you think about the ramp, whether you want it kept as it is, changed or reopened. We do not tell you what to say.

The ramp itself is in Senate District 30 and Assembly District 70. If you live in Morningside Heights, your Assembly district is 69, which the Assembly's website lists as Micah C. Lasher's. The transportation department named its contact for the ramp in its July 2025 closure notice. For other city services, call 311.

A few things make a letter easier to act on:

  • Say who you are and which neighbourhood you live in, and give your contact details.
  • Name the place exactly: the Henry Hudson Parkway northbound Exit 12N ramp to West 125th Street, at Riverside Park.
  • Say what you have seen, when and how often, and what it means for you. Photos help.
  • Ask for something specific: a review, the next steps, or which agency decides.
§5

Story How this came about, and what comes next

In October 2026 we, Brook Constantz and Eric Bolton of Spherical Harmonics, wrote to the neighbours, the community board and others who were already working on it, and offered a way for more people to take part: a shared map anyone could draw on.

On November 4 we held an online workshop on it. On Sunday, November 22, neighbours and street advocacy groups held a public walk from 116th Street and Riverside Drive to the ramp, with thirty-one people registered. Afterwards we held a workshop to work on the map together.

We plan to bring what the map shows to Community Board 9 in December.

§6

Thanks The people who shaped the map

This was started by people who live here and care about the ramp, and the map has been shaped by their time and knowledge. Thank you to the neighbours who have worked for years to make this ramp a place for people and who shared what they know, to the advocates and organisations who walked it with us and told their readers about it, to the thirty-one people who registered for the walk, and to everyone who joined a workshop, drew a proposal or cast a vote.

If we left someone out, or you would rather not be named here, tell us and we will change this page.
